# SALES STRATEGY

### 1. Lead Outreach

* Targeted lead outreach
* Main target: **HVAC businesses**

### 2. 20-Minute Discovery Call

Flow:

1. Introduce yourself
2. Know who they are / what their business does
3. Get down to business
4. Ask questions and understand their problems
5. Identify what they actually need
6. Present the solution based on what they said

### KEY TO CLOSE THE DEAL

**ASK QUESTIONS.**

The more na parang nagse-sell ka, mas lalong mahirap mag-close.

Instead of constantly explaining the offer, **let them talk.**

Need mo sila mapa-YES **3–5 times** naturally:

* “Does it make sense to you?”
* “Do you think this can help your business?”
* “Let me check if I understand you correctly…”
* “Is this the problem you're currently experiencing?”
* “Would solving this help your business?”

Goal: **YES → YES → YES → YES → YES**

Hindi dapat aggressive selling. Dapat parang **consultation/conversation.**
